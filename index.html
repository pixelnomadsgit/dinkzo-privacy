Dinkzo Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026 · Version 2026-10-04

Your privacy at a glance

Dinkzo helps players discover and organize pickleball events, manage courts and tournaments, communicate with event participants, and view results. We use the information needed for those features. Event records are shared records: hiding your profile statistics does not make the underlying match results confidential. You can change available settings or contact us about access, correction and deletion.

1. Who operates Dinkzo

Dinkzo is operated by Pixel Nomads, the personal information controller, based in Marigondon, Lapu-Lapu City, Cebu, Philippines. Contact our privacy representative at contact@pixelnomads.app.

This policy covers Dinkzo's web and mobile services and explains how we handle information under applicable privacy law, including the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012. Features vary by platform and version. External providers apply their own policies.

2. Information we handle

  • Account information: name, email address, account identifier, sign-in provider and authentication information. Firebase Authentication handles email/password and supported Google sign-in. A display name or username may initially be derived from your sign-in details, including the part of your email before the @ sign; review it before participating.
  • Player profiles and preferences: display name, username, biography, skill level, recreational or tournament preferences, singles/doubles preferences, selected city or place, playing schedule, notification choices, follow approval and statistics visibility.
  • Locations: foreground device coordinates used for nearby discovery; saved profile or venue locations, including address components, map coordinates and place identifiers; and search inputs used to find places.
  • Events, courts and tournaments: event details, hosting and official roles, join requests, invitations, participant names and identifiers, teams, divisions, attendance, check-in status, queue positions, court assignments, match pairings, scores, results, standings and event history.
  • Event fee records: fees, recorded amounts paid, payment status and waivers entered for event administration. These records are distinct from payment-provider confirmation.
  • Community and communication: club details and membership roles, follow relationships and requests, event-chat messages and sender details, message-read information, notifications, and feedback or support correspondence.
  • Technical information: session and local-storage information, app preferences, and connection or operational information needed to deliver and troubleshoot the service. Hosting, authentication and map providers may receive IP addresses, request information and device/browser details when serving requests.

Provide only information needed for a feature. Do not put passwords, payment credentials, medical information or another person's private contact details into profiles, event descriptions or chat. If you contact us or a payment recipient, we also receive the correspondence or transaction information you provide through that channel.

3. Location, camera and local storage

Nearby discovery uses your device's foreground location with permission. After permission has been granted, Dinkzo can obtain a current position again at startup without another prompt. The app can remember the position during the session. It does not provide continuous background location tracking.

You can deny or revoke permission in your device or browser settings and continue browsing events using other filters. A manually selected profile or venue location is different from a temporary device position: its address and coordinates can be saved with the relevant record and used for display, distance calculations and maps. Avoid selecting your home or another sensitive place as a shared venue.

The camera is used to scan event check-in QR codes where available. Check-in processes the scanned event information and your participation status; it is not a video-recording feature. You can control camera access through device settings.

Dinkzo and its authentication services use local storage or equivalent device storage for sessions, preferences and relevant caches. Clearing local data may sign you out or reset preferences; it does not delete cloud records. In-app event, invitation and reminder settings control the available notification features and do not by themselves establish that operating-system push notifications are enabled.

4. Purposes and processing grounds

We use account details to authenticate you; profile and location information for discovery and player preferences; event, court and participant records for organizing play; scores for results and statistics; and communication records for event chat, invitations and support. Fee records help organizers administer participation. Operational information supports reliability, moderation and security.

Depending on the purpose, processing of ordinary personal information rests on providing the requested service, legal obligations, consent or legitimate interests such as security, subject to your rights. Sensitive information requires a separately applicable legal ground. Consent, where relied on, can be withdrawn through the relevant control or by contacting us. Agreeing to the Terms does not give blanket permission for unrelated processing.

Pairings, queues, rankings and statistics are feature calculations, not medical assessments or decisions about credit, employment or insurance. Organizers may correct results and scores. Contact the organizer or us if information is wrong.

5. Information other people can see

Players may see names, relevant player details, shared locations, event participation, scores, standings, accepted follow relationships and club information through the features they can access. Event organizers and authorized officials use participation, check-in, fee and scoring information to administer play. Club administrators manage membership and club content. Event-chat messages are available to eligible participants in that event, rather than being private one-to-one messages.

Statistics visibility: Settings offers Everyone, People who follow you, and Nobody for profile record and match-history displays. The default is Everyone. Follow approval is a separate control. These settings control what Dinkzo's normal screens display; they do not make the underlying shared match records private. Signed-in users can access shared match records, and event results remain available for event operation. Do not rely on the Nobody setting as a guarantee that results cannot be reconstructed from those records.

Dinkzo does not provide a universal private-account setting that makes all event, match and club information invisible. A private club's membership controls do not automatically make every linked event or match confidential. Review a feature's audience before joining, posting or selecting a location.

Authorized administrators can access information necessary for service administration, moderation and support. Recipients can retain screenshots or copies outside Dinkzo; we cannot directly remove those copies.

6. Providers and disclosures

  • Google Firebase and Google Cloud: account authentication, application database storage and synchronization, and related infrastructure. See Firebase privacy information and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google sign-in: supplies the identity information authorized through that sign-in service. Your Google account remains subject to Google's terms and controls.
  • Google Maps and places services: process map, venue, place-search and location requests. Opening an external maps app passes the destination to that provider. See Google Maps terms and Google's privacy policy.
  • GCash: optional developer support uses an external GCash QR/app flow on supported platforms. Dinkzo does not receive your PIN, password or one-time code and does not receive automatic payment confirmation from this flow. The recipient may see transaction details supplied by GCash. See GCash's Privacy Notice.
  • Hosting and external websites: providers serving the app or these legal pages, including GitHub for repository-hosted pages, receive requests needed to deliver them. External event or provider links are governed by their own policies.

We may disclose relevant information when required by law or necessary and lawful to investigate abuse, protect people, or establish or defend claims. We limit disclosures to their purpose.

We do not sell personal information. Dinkzo does not currently operate a third-party in-app advertising program or intentionally use advertising SDKs to profile users across unrelated services. Necessary infrastructure and map processing still occurs.

7. Storage and security

Information is stored in cloud systems and, where needed, on your device. Providers may process information outside the Philippines; locations depend on the configured service. We remain responsible for information under our control and use appropriate provider arrangements and access controls.

Dinkzo uses authenticated accounts and database access rules. No system is completely secure, and not every display-visibility setting is a server-enforced confidentiality restriction, as explained above. We investigate security incidents and provide notifications required by law.

8. Retention

We retain account information while needed to provide the account and requested features. Event and match records support shared history, statistics and dispute resolution; account deletion requires assessing identifying information in those shared records rather than treating them as indefinitely exempt from erasure.

Our existing policy allows support and privacy correspondence to be retained for up to three years after a matter closes, unless a specific legal, security or dispute need requires longer retention. Technical records are kept only as needed for operation, security and applicable obligations.

Restricted recovery copies may remain after active-system deletion until their lifecycle expires. Our target maximum backup retention is 180 days, subject to the actual settings of the infrastructure service; this is a target, not a verified guarantee for every provider. Ask us for retention details relevant to your request. Recovery copies must not be used to resume unrelated processing of deleted information.

Where we retain information after a deletion request, we will explain the applicable purpose and retention criteria. Technical cleanup limitations do not justify indefinite retention.

9. Account deletion and requests without the app

Open Profile → Settings → Delete account and follow the confirmation shown. If a recent login is required, sign out, sign back in and retry. Dinkzo's confirmation flow may differ from other Pixel Nomads apps.

The current self-service process removes the authentication account after deleting covered account records, notifications, hosted events and matches booked by that account. It does not automatically erase every shared or separately stored record. Matches you participated in can remain with names and participant identifiers to preserve other players' records; follows, chat, club records and other identifying references may require additional cleanup.

For complete account-and-personal-data deletion, or if the app flow fails, email contact@pixelnomads.app with the subject Dinkzo Account Deletion. You do not need to install or open Dinkzo. Identify your account, preferably using its email address, and specify complete deletion or the particular records concerned. Do not send a password, PIN or one-time code. We may verify ownership proportionately.

We aim to complete verified requests within 30 days, subject to identity verification, lawful retention requirements and request complexity. We will explain any delay or limitation; applicable legal deadlines still apply. Shared-record cleanup may require administrator review, including removal or de-identification of your information while protecting other people's legitimate records. This does not make all shared content exempt from deletion.

If you manage an event or club, tell us about any transfer or closure needed. Deleting your account is not an automatic fee refund or cancellation with an external venue. Uninstalling Dinkzo does not delete your account. Copies independently held by recipients or payment providers are subject to their own controls.

10. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request information about processing, access, correction, objection, erasure or blocking, portability and withdrawal of consent. Email our privacy representative to exercise these rights. We will explain any lawful restriction. You may complain to the Philippine National Privacy Commission and seek remedies available under law.

11. Younger users

Dinkzo's current account policy is intended for users aged 18 or older. An under-18 person must not independently create or maintain an account without the legally valid authorization and arrangements required under applicable law. A parent or legal guardian should contact us before providing a child's information or arranging account access.

This policy does not claim that Dinkzo has an automated age-verification or verified parental-consent system. Contact us if a child has provided information contrary to these requirements. We will investigate and arrange appropriate restriction, correction or deletion, respecting applicable rights. An organizer's youth event or age division does not itself authorize creation of a child's Dinkzo account.
