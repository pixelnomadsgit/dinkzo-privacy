1. About Dinkzo and This Policy

Dinkzo is a pickleball application operated by PixelNomads in the Philippines. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose, and delete personal information when you use Dinkzo or contact us.

The personal information controller responsible for Dinkzo is PixelNomads ("PixelNomads," "we," "us," or "our").

Our business correspondence address is [INSERT YOUR OFFICIAL BUSINESS ADDRESS], Philippines. You can contact our privacy representative using the information provided in Section 13.

We handle personal information in accordance with applicable Philippine privacy law, including Republic Act No. 10173, or the Data Privacy Act of 2012, its implementing rules and regulations, and applicable issuances of the National Privacy Commission.

Under the Data Privacy Act, individuals have rights concerning their personal information, including rights to be informed, access, correction, objection, erasure or blocking, data portability, and complaint, subject to applicable legal conditions and limitations.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, information generated when you use Dinkzo, and limited technical information necessary to operate, secure, and maintain the application.

Account and Profile Information

Depending on the account and profile features you use, Dinkzo may collect and store:

  • Display name or name provided by you.
  • Email address associated with your account.
  • Account identifier generated by the authentication system.
  • Profile photo, if you choose to provide one.
  • Selected city or general location displayed on your profile.
  • Pickleball-related profile information that you choose to provide.
  • Player preferences and other profile information entered in the app.
  • Account authentication information required to sign in.

Some profile information is required to create or maintain your account, while other information is optional and depends on the features you choose to use.

Pickleball Activity and User Content

When you participate in Dinkzo activities, we may store information associated with those activities, including:

  • Events you create, submit, join, or organize.
  • Event registration and participation status.
  • Player and participant information associated with events.
  • Match participation and match status.
  • Scores and match results.
  • Wins, losses, match totals, rankings, and related statistics.
  • Tournament participation and bracket information.
  • Club or group membership and related activity.
  • Followers and following relationships.
  • Messages sent through event or community communication features.
  • Feedback, reports, and other content you voluntarily submit.

Dinkzo does not require you to provide private contact information in public or shared content. You should avoid placing information such as personal phone numbers, passwords, financial information, or other sensitive information into content intended for other users.

Support and Privacy Requests

When you contact us for support, privacy requests, account deletion, feedback, or other assistance, we may receive your email address, correspondence, account information, and supporting information you choose to provide.

We use this information to authenticate and process your request, communicate with you, investigate reported issues, and provide support.

Technical Information

Dinkzo and the infrastructure supporting it may process limited technical information necessary to authenticate users, deliver notifications, protect the service, diagnose technical problems, and maintain service reliability.

This may include:

  • IP address or network information processed by service providers.
  • Device or installation identifiers.
  • Operating system and device information.
  • Application version.
  • Authentication timestamps and security-related information.
  • Firebase authentication and installation identifiers.
  • Information associated with push-notification delivery.
  • Technical logs generated when the application communicates with our services.

We use technical information for authentication, security, abuse prevention, troubleshooting, service operation, and maintaining the reliability of Dinkzo.

Location and Device Permissions

Dinkzo may request access to your device location when you use features that require location information, such as selecting or identifying a nearby location or venue.

Location access is used to support location-based features and is not intended to continuously track your physical movements.

Dinkzo does not request background location access for continuous location tracking. Location access is used only when a feature requiring location information is actively used and permission has been granted.

Where a location is saved to your Dinkzo profile or associated with an event, the selected location may be stored as part of that profile or event information. Dinkzo does not require your precise residential address to create a player profile.

Maps and Venue Location

Dinkzo uses Google Maps and related Google location services to support venue selection, map display, and location-based event functionality. When you search for or select a venue, the information necessary to perform that request may be processed by Google according to Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

Dinkzo may store the selected venue information and geographic coordinates associated with an event so that other authorized users can identify the event location.

Notifications

Dinkzo may request permission to send push notifications. Notifications may be used for account-related messages, event updates, event participation, match activity, messages, and other features you use.

You can disable notification permissions through your device settings. Disabling notifications may prevent you from receiving timely alerts from Dinkzo, but does not normally prevent you from using other parts of the application.

Photos and Media

If Dinkzo provides a feature allowing you to select or upload a profile photo or other media, the application may request access to your device photo library or camera as required by that feature.

Access is used only for the feature for which you grant permission. You can withdraw or change these permissions through your device settings. Removing permission may prevent the affected feature from functioning.

Dinkzo does not require access to your contacts or calendar for ordinary account, event, tournament, or player functionality.

3. How and Why We Use Information

We use personal information to provide and operate Dinkzo, maintain your account, store and synchronize your information, coordinate events and matches, communicate with you, protect the service, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Depending on the feature you use, we may use information to:

  • Create and maintain your Dinkzo account.
  • Authenticate your identity and provide account access.
  • Maintain your player profile.
  • Display relevant player and event information.
  • Allow players to discover and participate in pickleball events.
  • Allow organizers to create and manage events.
  • Coordinate participants, matches, courts, and tournaments.
  • Record and display match scores and results.
  • Calculate or display player statistics and rankings.
  • Support clubs, groups, followers, and community features.
  • Send notifications relating to features you use.
  • Provide event and participant communication features.
  • Respond to support requests and feedback.
  • Detect, prevent, and investigate misuse or unauthorized activity.
  • Maintain and improve the security and reliability of Dinkzo.
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements.

We do not use your personal information for a purpose that is incompatible with the purpose for which it was collected unless permitted or required by applicable law or otherwise authorized by you.

Depending on the processing involved, our legal basis may include performance of our agreement with you, compliance with a legal obligation, your consent where required, or legitimate interests such as maintaining service security and preventing abuse, provided that applicable privacy requirements are satisfied.

Where consent is required, we obtain consent for the relevant purpose. You may withdraw consent by contacting us and, where applicable, changing your app or device settings. Withdrawal does not invalidate processing lawfully carried out before withdrawal.

Dinkzo does not intentionally collect sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for a specific feature and permitted under applicable law.

4. Information Visible to Other People

Dinkzo is a social and event-based pickleball application. Some information is therefore intentionally displayed to other users as part of profiles, events, matches, tournaments, clubs, groups, and communication features.

Information Visible to Other Players

Depending on the feature being used, other Dinkzo users may be able to see:

  • Your display name.
  • Your profile photo, if you have provided one.
  • Your selected city or general location shown on your profile.
  • Player information displayed on your profile.
  • Your participation in events visible to those users.
  • Your player status within an event or match.
  • Your match results and scores when displayed by an event.
  • Your wins, losses, match totals, or rankings where displayed.
  • Your club or group membership where displayed.
  • Your followers or following relationships where displayed.
  • Content you voluntarily post or send through shared features.

Information Visible to Event Organizers

Event organizers may access information needed to administer an event, including:

  • Your display name and profile photo.
  • Your relevant player profile information.
  • Your registration and participation status.
  • Your payment status or amount paid where applicable.
  • Your match participation and results.
  • Your scores and player statistics relevant to the event.
  • Your status within courts, matches, or tournament brackets.
  • Messages you send through event-related communication features.

Information Visible to Club or Group Administrators

If you join a Dinkzo club or group, administrators may be able to view information associated with your membership and activity within that club or group, including profile information and content made available to members.

Information Visible to Message Recipients

Messages, chats, feedback, and other communications you intentionally send to another person may be visible to the intended recipient and, where applicable, other participants in the relevant conversation or event.

Default Visibility

Information displayed through a public profile, event, match, tournament, club, group, ranking, or community feature may be visible to the Dinkzo users who have access to that feature.

Dinkzo does not currently provide a universal "private profile" setting that makes all profile and activity information invisible to other users. Any visibility controls provided for a specific feature apply only to that feature.

Information you share with other people may be copied, photographed, screenshotted, saved, or otherwise retained by them outside Dinkzo. Dinkzo cannot control information once another person has obtained it.

Do not include private contact details, passwords, financial information, authentication credentials, or other sensitive information in content intended for a public or shared audience.

5. Service Providers and Other Disclosures

Dinkzo uses third-party service providers to operate essential parts of the application. These providers process information only as necessary to provide their services, maintain security, or comply with applicable requirements.

Google Firebase

Dinkzo uses Google Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, to support account authentication, application data storage, synchronization, and related backend functionality.

Where enabled in the application, Firebase Cloud Messaging may process device or installation identifiers and notification-related information to deliver push notifications.

We configure Firebase services according to the needs of Dinkzo. We do not assume that every Firebase product is enabled merely because the application uses Firebase.

For additional information about Firebase's privacy practices, see Firebase Privacy and Security .

Google Maps and Location Services

Dinkzo uses Google Maps and related Google services to support venue search, map display, and location selection. Information required to perform these requests may be processed by Google in accordance with Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

Google's privacy practices are described in its Privacy Policy .

Authentication Providers

Dinkzo may provide authentication through email/password and supported third-party sign-in providers, including Google Sign-In where enabled. Authentication providers may receive information necessary to authenticate your account and return the authentication result to Dinkzo.

Other Disclosures

We may disclose relevant information when required by law, valid legal process, court order, or governmental request. We may also disclose limited information where necessary and lawful to protect users, investigate misuse, enforce our terms, protect our rights, or prevent fraud and security threats.

We limit such disclosures to information reasonably appropriate for the relevant purpose.

Advertising, Tracking, and Sale of Information

Dinkzo does not sell personal information to third parties.

Dinkzo does not currently operate a third-party advertising program within the application and does not intentionally use advertising SDKs to create advertising profiles of users.

Dinkzo does not intentionally track users across unrelated third-party applications or websites for advertising purposes.

This statement does not prevent technical processing performed by infrastructure providers, authentication services, maps services, or other providers necessary to operate Dinkzo.

6. Storage and International Processing

Dinkzo uses cloud infrastructure rather than storing all information exclusively on your device.

Dinkzo's backend data is stored and processed using Google Firebase and related Google Cloud infrastructure. The geographic location of processing depends on the Firebase service and the configured resource location.

Personal information submitted to Dinkzo may therefore be processed outside the Philippines, including in countries where our service providers or their infrastructure operate.

We use appropriate contractual, organizational, and technical safeguards when engaging service providers and when personal information is processed across borders.

7. How Long We Retain Information

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Dinkzo, maintain security, fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Account and Profile Information

Account and profile information is generally retained while your Dinkzo account remains active. When you request account deletion, we delete the information from our active systems subject to lawful retention requirements and technical deletion cycles.

Events, Matches, Scores, and Tournament Records

Event and match information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide event history, player statistics, rankings, tournament records, dispute resolution, security, and other legitimate Dinkzo functionality.

Where deletion of a user account affects shared event or match records, we may remove or de-identify personal identifiers where reasonably practicable while preserving records necessary for the integrity of other users' event or match history.

Support and Privacy Requests

Support correspondence and privacy requests may be retained for up to three (3) years after the matter is closed, unless a longer period is required for legal, security, or dispute-resolution purposes.

Technical and Security Information

Technical and security information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for security, troubleshooting, abuse prevention, service operation, and legal compliance.

Backups

Deleted information may temporarily remain in encrypted backups or disaster-recovery systems until those backups are automatically overwritten or expire according to the applicable backup lifecycle.

Backup copies are not restored or used for ordinary application operations except where necessary for disaster recovery, security, or legal purposes.

Our target maximum backup retention period is 180 days, subject to the actual retention settings of the applicable infrastructure provider.

Information retained after account deletion is limited to information necessary for legal compliance, fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution, or the integrity of shared records.

8. Account and Personal-Data Deletion

Request Deletion in Dinkzo

To request deletion of your Dinkzo account, open the application and navigate to:

Profile → Settings → Account → Delete Account

Follow the confirmation steps displayed by Dinkzo.

Request Deletion Without the App

If you cannot access Dinkzo, you may request account deletion by contacting our privacy representative at:

[INSERT YOUR MONITORED PRIVACY EMAIL]

Use the subject line "Dinkzo Account Deletion" and provide enough information for us to identify the account. Do not send your password or authentication credentials.

We may request limited information needed to verify account ownership before processing the deletion request.

What Deletion Covers

A verified account-deletion request is intended to permanently remove your account and associated personal information from our active systems, subject to the retention exceptions described in Section 7.

Where applicable, deletion includes:

  • Firebase authentication account information.
  • Dinkzo profile information.
  • Personal account records stored in Cloud Firestore.
  • Profile media stored by Dinkzo.
  • Personal identifiers associated with user-generated content where deletion is technically supported.

Some shared event, match, tournament, or ranking records may need to be retained to preserve the integrity of records belonging to other users. Where reasonably practicable, personally identifying information will be removed or de-identified from such records.

Messages or other content that has already been delivered to another user may remain in that user's possession or device after your account is deleted. Dinkzo cannot delete copies that another user has already downloaded, screenshotted, or otherwise retained outside Dinkzo.

We aim to complete verified deletion requests within 30 days, subject to identity verification, technical requirements, lawful retention obligations, and the complexity of the request.

Information held in backups may remain until the applicable backup retention period expires, as described in Section 7.

Uninstalling Dinkzo from your device does not delete your Dinkzo account or cloud-stored information.

10. Information Security

We use reasonable organizational, physical, and technical safeguards appropriate to the information we process.

These safeguards include, where applicable:

  • Encrypted communication between the application and backend services.
  • Authenticated access to user accounts.
  • Access controls for application data.
  • Firebase Authentication for account authentication.
  • Cloud Firestore Security Rules for database access control.
  • Restricted administrative access to backend systems.
  • Monitoring and investigation of suspicious or unauthorized activity.
  • Reasonable procedures for responding to security incidents.

No electronic system, internet transmission, or storage system can guarantee absolute security. We therefore cannot guarantee that your information will never be accessed, disclosed, altered, or destroyed through a security incident.

If a personal data breach occurs, we will assess the incident and take appropriate steps, including notifying affected individuals and the appropriate authorities where required by applicable law.
